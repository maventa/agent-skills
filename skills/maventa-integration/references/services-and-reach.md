## Services & Reach

- [Maventa services and reach](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/overview.md): See where Maventa delivers e-invoices across Europe: supported networks per country, cross-border routing over Peppol and local networks, and Peppol identifier schemes.
- [Upcoming changes](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/upcoming-changes.md): Plan ahead with upcoming e-invoicing regulation dates and Maventa service changes in each country Maventa serves, listed by year.

## Finland

- [e-invoicing in Finland](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/finland.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Finland through Maventa: operator and bank networks, Finvoice and TEAPPSXML, OVT codes, Peppol, OmaPosti consumer invoicing and scanning.
- [Mass Printing Service](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/finland/mass-printing-service.md): Send PDF letters such as payslips as printed post or to OmaPosti in Finland with the Mass Printing Service API: account setup, sending and status checks.

## Sweden

- [e-invoicing in Sweden](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/sweden.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Sweden through Maventa: Peppol, the Swedish operator network and InExchange, the NEA registry, Svefaktura, and Kivra and Billo for consumers.

## Norway

- [e-invoicing in Norway](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/norway.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Norway through Maventa: mandatory B2B e-invoicing, Peppol and EHF, ELMA registration, and consumer invoicing via eFaktura, Vipps and AvtaleGiro.

## Denmark

- [e-invoicing in Denmark](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/denmark.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Denmark through Maventa: Nemhandel via Sproom, Peppol, OIOUBL, invoice responses, and registering end-customers to Nemhandel from March 2027.

## Netherlands

- [e-invoicing in the Netherlands](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/netherlands.md): Send and receive e-invoices in the Netherlands through Maventa: Peppol with KVK, OIN and VAT identifiers, SI-UBL 2.0 (NLCIUS), G-Account and scanning.

## Belgium

- [e-invoicing in Belgium](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/belgium.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Belgium, where B2B e-invoicing is mandatory from 2026: Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, CBE/KBO numbers with scheme 0208, scanning and self-billing.

## France

- [e-invoicing in France](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/france.md): Prepare for the French e-invoicing mandate with Maventa: receiving via partner platform MySupply, SIREN and SIRET, the Annuaire, EN16931-FR and Factur-X, lifecycle statuses.

## Germany

- [e-invoicing in Germany](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/germany.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Germany through Maventa: the phased German e-invoicing mandate, Peppol, XRechnung for domestic traffic, and ZuGFeRD by email.

## Poland

- [e-invoicing in Poland](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/poland.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Poland through KSeF with Maventa: KSeF tokens, the FA(3) format, UPO confirmations, and cross-border Peppol invoices reported to KSeF.

## Italy

- [e-invoicing in Italy](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/italy.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Italy through SDI with Maventa: Codice Destinatario setup, the FatturaPA format, SDI outcomes, and cross-border sending over Peppol.

## Iceland

- [e-invoicing in Iceland](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/iceland.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Iceland through Maventa: plain Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, kennitala registration and registry check, Peppol scheme 0196, and AutoScan.

## Estonia

- [e-invoicing in Estonia](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/estonia.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Estonia through Maventa: registrikood or VAT number registration, Peppol schemes 0191 and 9931, email and print fallback, and AutoScan.

## Latvia

- [e-invoicing in Latvia](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/latvia.md): Send and receive e-invoices in Latvia through Maventa ahead of the 2028 B2B mandate: VAT number registration, Peppol scheme 9939, Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 and AutoScan.

## Slovakia

- [e-invoicing in Slovakia](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/slovakia.md): Check the state of e-invoicing in Slovakia and the 2027 domestic Peppol mandate. Maventa support for Slovakia is in development, planned for the end of 2026.

## Lithuania

- [e-invoicing in Lithuania](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/lithuania.md): Register Lithuanian companies in Maventa with the legal entity code and send e-invoices over Peppol, including B2G via SABIS. Peppol receiving follows in early 2027.

## Spain

- [e-invoicing in Spain](https://documentation.maventa.com/services-and-reach/spain.md): Check the state of e-invoicing in Spain: B2G via FACe since 2015 and the planned Crea y Crece B2B mandate. Maventa support for Spain is not yet decided.
